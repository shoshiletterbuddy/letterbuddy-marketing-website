The LetterBuddy approach

I do → We do → You do

A gentle progression gives students the support they need, then lets them show what they can do.

01

Model

Before a student writes a new letter, LetterBuddy plays a short, clear video showing how it is formed.

02

Trace & try

A faint tracing guide creates a visual track to follow. If a student wanders too far, they get a gentle prompt to try again.

03

Try independently

After tracing twice, the guide disappears, so students can practice on their own. Need it again? Turn it back on anytime.

Why it matters

Strong foundations make room for bigger ideas.

When forming letters becomes more automatic, students can put more energy into expressing what they want to say.

Build efficient habitsIt’s easier to establish helpful motor patterns early than to unlearn inefficient ones later.
Support the adults, tooParents, caregivers, and educators can see how each letter is being formed and spot where support is helpful.
Make practice feel manageableClear steps and immediate feedback help turn a big skill into a series of small wins.
Practice anywhereUse LetterBuddy on a computer, tablet, or phone, wherever your student is ready to practice.
